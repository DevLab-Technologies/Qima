## 1. The product

### What it is

Qima is a personal tracker for what you own, built around precious metals first. It prices gold, silver, platinum and palladium alongside crypto, stocks, indices and currencies, and adds everything up to one number in the currency you choose.

| Job the user hires it for | Evidence |
|---|---|
| See live metal prices in the unit and purity they buy in (oz t, g, kg; 24K, 22K, 21K, 18K) | `lib/models/asset.dart:68` (`PriceUnit`), store description "BUILT FOR BULLION" |
| Track a watchlist of mixed assets, same asset in several currencies or units | `README.md` "Watchlist"; screenshot `en-US/iphone_1_watchlist` shows Gold in USD/oz t and Gold in SAR/g/21K |
| Record buys, sells, transfers and fees; see value, cost, average cost, realised and unrealised gain | `onboardingStep3Body`, release notes 3.0.0 |
| See the whole portfolio, its chart and allocation (by class, gold by karat, drift since January) | `allocationTitle`, `allocationDriftSince`, release notes |
| Work out zakat: nisab (85 g gold or 595 g silver), what counts, hawl date on Hijri or Gregorian calendar, reminder | `helpZakatStep1Body`, `zakatCalendarHijri`, `lib/models/zakat.dart` |
| Get price alerts (above, below, % move in 24 h or 7 days) | `alertKind*`, `alertWindow*` |
| Keep data private: no account, app lock, hide balances, encrypted backup (AES-256), CSV import and export | `onboardingStep5*`, `backupExportSheetProtectSubtitle` |
| Share an asset card as an image | `lib/screens/share_asset_sheet.dart`, `lib/widgets/asset_share_card.dart` (uncommitted work on this branch) |
| Glance at prices on the Home and Lock Screen | `onboardingStep4Body`, `lib/services/home_widget_service.dart` |

### Coverage (built-in catalog, `lib/models/instrument_catalog.dart`)

| Class | Built in |
|---|---|
| Metals | XAU, XAG, XPT, XPD |
| Crypto | BTC, ETH |
| Stocks | AAPL, MSFT, NVDA, AMZN, TSLA, GOOGL (plus any custom ticker, validated live) |
| Indices | S&P 500, Dow Jones, Nasdaq, Russell 2000, FTSE 100, Nikkei 225, DAX |

Price sources: `api.gold-api.com` (metals, crypto spot), Yahoo Finance's unofficial chart endpoint (stocks, indices, history), `open.er-api.com` (FX). The README itself warns that Yahoo's endpoint "carries no stability or terms-of-service guarantee".

### Audience (inferred, needs confirming)

The product points strongly at people in the Gulf and wider Arab world who hold physical gold, with a secondary international audience:

- Gold by karat "the way Gulf and Middle-East jewellery markets quote it" (`README.md`).
- Eight of the eleven built-in currencies are Gulf or Levant/Egypt currencies.
- A full zakat module with Hijri calendar and scholar-deferring copy.
- A bundled font just for the Saudi Riyal sign (`assets/fonts/riyal`).
- Arabic is a first-class language with full RTL and its own font (Almarai).

Drift from that voice:

- Contractions are inconsistent: "Couldn't refresh" (7) vs "Could not verify" (8).

## 4. Constraints

### Name: how the product writes itself today

| Context | Written as | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Home screen label, all languages | Qima | `ios/Runner/Info.plist` `CFBundleDisplayName`, `AndroidManifest.xml` `android:label`, `macos/.../AppInfo.xcconfig` |
| In-app title, English, Spanish, French | Qima | `appTitle` |
| In-app title, Arabic | قيمة | `app_ar.arb:3` |
| Inside Arabic sentences | Qima (Latin), 30 strings | e.g. `app_ar.arb:127` "Qima مقفل" |
| App Store name, English | Qima: Gold & Portfolio | `en-US/name.txt` |
| App Store name, Arabic | قيمة: الذهب والمحفظة | `ar-SA/name.txt` |
| Play Store name, French | Qima : Or et Portefeuille (French spacing) | `android/.../fr-FR/title.txt` |
| Internal bundle name | qima (lower case) | `CFBundleName` |
| Meaning | Arabic قيمة, "value" | `README.md` line 3 |

Meaning: confirmed in the repo, and linguistically correct. قيمة (qīma) is the standard Arabic word for value or worth. Pronunciation: KEE-ma, with an Arabic q (a uvular sound made further back than k); most English speakers will say "KEE-mah". There is no pronunciation guide anywhere in the repo.

Problems:

- N1. Arabic copy writes the name two ways: قيمة as the title, Latin "Qima" in 30 other strings.
- N2. قيمة is also an ordinary word the Arabic UI uses for "value" (`holdingsValue` "القيمة", `portfolioValue` "القيمة الإجمالية", `settingsBaseCurrencyFooter` "...إجمالي قيمة محفظتك"). Using قيمة as the brand name in Arabic can make "Qima" and "value" collide in the same screen.
- N3. The home-screen label is "Qima" even on Arabic devices, while the Arabic store listing says قيمة. Search and recognition may split.

### Right to left and Arabic script

- Full RTL layout via Flutter `Directionality`; screenshot `ar-SA/iphone_1_watchlist` mirrors correctly.
- Charts and instrument glyphs are pinned LTR on purpose (`price_chart.dart:355`, `instrument_icon.dart:44`).
- Arabic small text is boosted 1.5 pt and loses letter spacing (`DS.overline`). Good practice, worth keeping.
- Digits are mixed on one screen: prices use Western digits ("$4,060.80") while localised labels use Eastern Arabic digits ("٣ أشهر", "٢١ قيراط"). No rule exists.
- Almarai includes Latin glyphs, so in Arabic mode tickers and prices render in Almarai; in other locales they render in SF or Roboto. The same figure looks different by language.

### Platform rules

- iOS: 1024x1024 master, no alpha; met. iOS 18 dark and tinted icon variants: not found in the repo.
- Android: adaptive icon with background `#0B0C10` and a 16% foreground inset. No monochrome (themed) icon layer found.
- macOS and Windows: rounded-square master `app_icon_macos.png`.
- Play feature graphic exists for en-US and ar.
- Store screenshots: iPhone and iPad, English and Arabic only; none for Spanish, French, Mac or Android phones beyond two files.

### Accessibility

Measured contrast of current text tokens (WCAG 2.x relative luminance):

| Pair | Dark | Light |
|---|---|---|
| textPrimary on tile (worst surface) | 13.39 | 16.75 |
| textSecondary on tile | 5.89 | 6.62 |
| textTertiary on tile | 4.81 | 4.67 |
| up on tile | 5.60 | 5.96 |
| down on tile | 4.80 | 5.74 |
| brandText on tile | 7.33 | 4.84 |
| onBrand on brand | 10.90 | 8.17 |

All text tokens pass AA 4.5:1. `test/theme/design_system_contrast_test.dart` already enforces this, which is worth keeping.

Gaps:

- A1. Light `brand` gold as a UI indicator is 2.24:1 on white cards, below the 3:1 non-text minimum (WCAG 1.4.11). It marks the selected range chip and filter chip and the on-state of switches.
- A2. Light accents for crypto (2.79:1), platinum (2.89:1) and fiat (3.14:1) on tiles are at or below 3:1. Fine as tints, not as the only way to tell series apart (allocation chart).
- A3. Dark `accentIndex` is 3.81:1 on tiles; fine for icons, fails as text.
- A4. Only 12 `Semantics`, `semanticLabel` or `tooltip` uses across the app. Icon-only buttons (help, refresh, add, bell, tune) need audit.
- A5. No reduced-motion handling.
- A6. Gains and losses are told apart by colour plus an arrow glyph in rows (good), but chart lines rely on colour alone.
- A7. Touch targets not audited yet (range chips look about 32 pt tall in screenshots; iOS minimum 44, Material 48).

## 5. Open questions for the owner

Please answer each in one line. Letters in brackets are my suggested default if you have no preference.

1. Positioning: is Qima "a gold-first portfolio tracker" (metals lead, everything else follows) or "one private place for everything you own" (all classes equal)? [gold-first]
2. Primary audience: Arab and Gulf gold holders first, with a global secondary audience? Or global first? [Arab and Gulf first]
3. Is zakat a headline feature in marketing, or a feature listed among others? [headline in Arabic, listed in other languages]
4. Tone range: may the brand ever be playful, or always calm and plain? [always calm and plain]
5. Name in Arabic: keep قيمة as the Arabic brand name, or write "Qima" in Latin everywhere, including Arabic? (See N2: قيمة is also the everyday word for "value" in the UI.) [Qima in Latin inside sentences, قيمة only in store titles]
6. Should the home-screen label on Arabic devices read قيمة? [yes]
7. Logo: do you want a wordmark designed, or should the app icon plus "Qima" set in a chosen typeface stay the logo? [a simple wordmark in an existing open font, no custom drawing]
8. App icon: keep the rising-arrow chart, refine it (same idea, cleaner), or redraw it around the name or metal? [refine; an arrow that only goes up implies gains]
9. Latin brand typeface: stay on platform fonts (SF Pro, Roboto) in the app and pick one open font only for marketing, or ship a brand font in the app? [platform fonts in app, one open font for marketing]
10. Arabic digits: Western digits everywhere (as prices do now), or Eastern Arabic digits in Arabic? [Western everywhere, for numbers that match bank statements]
11. Do you want a tagline? If yes, is "Every asset you hold. One number." the one to keep? [keep it]
12. "Live prices": may we keep saying "live", or should copy say "up-to-date" or "near-live"? (Legal weight: sources may be delayed.) [keep "live" with the existing delay disclaimer]
13. Security claims: may we name AES-256 in marketing? Any certification or audit we can cite? [AES-256 yes; no certifications]
14. Yahoo Finance: the README says swap it for a licensed feed before commercial distribution. Is that done or planned? It affects whether we can advertise stock and index prices.
15. Wear OS: does it ship? If not, should the README claim go?
16. Tola: add the unit to the app, or remove it from the website? [remove from site]
17. iOS widgets: shipped in 3.0.0? (The site says "coming".)
18. Free forever: may the guidelines promise "free, no subscription", or only "free today"? [only state it as current fact]
19. Mode in marketing: dark-first, or show light and dark? [dark-first, light where it helps]
20. Publisher line: "by DevLab Technologies" on store and site, or Qima stands alone? [stands alone; publisher in footer only]
21. Scope of Phase 2 assets: do you want the website (`site/`) brought in line too, or app and store only? [include the site]
