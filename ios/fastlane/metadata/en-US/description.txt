Qima adds up everything you own and everything you owe into one number, in the currency you choose: bank accounts, cash, cards, loans, gold, crypto, property and money between people.

YOUR NET WORTH
• Net worth day by day, with a chart from one week to all time.
• A balance sheet of what you own and what you owe.
• Value property, cars and other assets yourself. Each value keeps its date, and nothing is made up in between.

EVERYDAY MONEY
• Bank accounts, cash, credit cards and loans, each with its balance over time.
• Spending by category, a monthly budget and recurring payments.
• Add a transaction by typing it or saying it.
• Import bank statements as PDF, spreadsheet or screenshot. They're read on your device, and you check every row before it's added.
• Bring in bank text messages through a Shortcuts automation.
• Check a balance against your bank and choose how a difference comes in: as a transaction or as a new opening balance.

PEOPLE
• Money you lent and borrowed, with the net at a glance.
• Split a bill and keep only your share as spending.

GOLD AND PRICES
• Gold, silver, platinum and palladium per troy ounce, gram or kilogram, with 24K, 22K, 21K and 18K.
• Bitcoin and Ethereum, stocks, indices and world currencies beside your metals.
• Record each buy, sell, transfer and fee, and see value, average cost and gain.
• Price alerts, and widgets for the Home Screen and Lock Screen.

ZAKAT
• The zakat to pay over your whole balance sheet: holdings, cash, money owed to you and assets held for sale, less debts due.
• Nisab on 85 g of gold or 595 g of silver, and a hawl counted in lunar years, with a reminder.
• Qima calculates; the choices behind the calculation are yours.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account, no sign-up, no ads, no analytics, no tracking.
• Your data stays on your devices.
• Lock the app with Face ID or Touch ID, hide balances, and back up to a file you can protect with a password (AES-256).

English, Arabic, Spanish and French, with full right-to-left Arabic. Free, with no subscription and no in-app purchases.
