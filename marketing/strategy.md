# Qima marketing strategy

Status: final strategy, built from the Phase 1 proposal with every suggested default accepted by the owner (questions 1 to 14). Nothing has been posted, sent or published. Prepared 1 November 2026.

Sources: `brand/marketing-agent-prompt.md`, `brand/guidelines.md` (§1 claims table, §8 voice, §10 applications), `brand/open-questions.md`, `brand/discovery.md`, `site/index.html`, `ios/fastlane/metadata/*`, the public App Store page (checked 31 October 2026).

Companion files: [`calendar.md`](calendar.md), [`posts/`](posts/), [`measurement.md`](measurement.md), [`open-questions.md`](open-questions.md).

---

## 1. Objective (90 days)

1. **Installs** from people in Saudi Arabia, the Gulf and Egypt who hold physical gold.
2. **Builder reputation** for the owner, as a side effect of telling the building story in public.

Nothing is measured inside the app. Weeks 1 and 2 set a baseline from store and platform analytics. Targets are set from that baseline in week 3, not guessed now (see `measurement.md`).

## 2. The gate: week 0

The first post with a store link waits until both of these are true:

- 3.1.0 is live on the App Store (on 31 October 2026 the US page still showed 3.0.0).
- The corrected store text is live: subtitle, promotional text and description, in all four languages. The live US subtitle is still "Metals, crypto & stocks prices", which gives stocks headline weight (answer 14).

Also in week 0: create campaign links (see section 8). qima.money already shows the corrected copy and linked store buttons (checked 31 October 2026).

Until the owner confirms Google Play and Mac are live (open question O3), posts name **iPhone and iPad** only and link to the App Store only.

## 3. Phases

| Weeks | Phase | Focus |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Gate | Store upload, campaign links, assets ready. No posts with links |
| 1 to 8 | Founder story and product proof | One product decision a week: gold-first, karat and units, no accounts, one total, right-to-left Arabic, alerts and widgets, what was cut |
| 9 to 12 | Zakat run-up | Nisab, hawl, the reminder; ends just before Ramadan 1448, expected to begin around 8 February 2027 (astronomical estimate; the actual date depends on moon sighting) |

The calendar assumes week 1 starts no earlier than Monday 9 November 2026. If the gate slips, every week shifts with it. Weeks 9 to 12 should still end before Ramadan, so if the gate slips past 16 November, shorten weeks 1 to 8, not the zakat run-up.

## 6. Founder voice

- First person, from the person who built Qima. Every post says so ("I built", "أنا اللي عامل").
- The building story is the content: why gold first, why no accounts, how right-to-left Arabic was handled, what was cut.
- Same rules as the brand: precise, calm, plain, no exclamation marks, no hype, no emoji (rule 7; the owner uses emoji today, so posts leave them out).
- Arabic posts are in Egyptian dialect (owner decision, 31 October 2026). Write Qima in Latin letters inside sentences. Fiqh terms stay as they are usually written: نصاب، حول، زكاة.
- Never: returns, profits, "grow your wealth", what to buy; "real-time", "bank-grade", "military-grade"; "certified", "trusted by", user counts, ratings, testimonials, partner names; anything that sounds like a ruling on zakat; "your data never leaves your phone".

| **Total** | | | | **about 4.5** |
| Store and site | Where every post lands | Upload the corrected listings (already in the repo); create campaign links | Once in week 0, then a monthly check | one-time 2 to 3 hours |

Suggested posting slots (Riyadh time): X Arabic posts Sunday and Tuesday evening, X English post Thursday afternoon, LinkedIn Tuesday or Wednesday 8 to 10 am. Adjust from X and LinkedIn analytics after week 3.

Replies on X: answer real questions only, say "I built Qima" whenever it comes up, no cold mentions, no DMs. Reddit is handled separately by `grok-reddit-monitor-prompt.md`.

Dedicated Qima accounts: none for 90 days. Reserve a Qima handle on X so nobody else takes it, but don't post from it (open question O6).

No paid budget for 90 days. Revisit Apple Search Ads after the week-3 baseline.

## 10. Other low-cost channels (optional)

| Channel | Fit | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Store listing upload and optimisation (EN and AR) | Strong; do first | Every channel lands here, and the live US listing still shows the old copy |
| Ramadan and zakat-season timing | Strong | The primary audience's strongest seasonal reason to care; weeks 9 to 12 |
| Arabic content | Strong | The primary audience, and it matches the owner's existing voice |
| Reddit and forums, with ownership disclosed | Medium | The right people, but strict self-promotion rules. Answer real questions only (the Grok drafter already exists) |
| Flutter community (right-to-left and theming write-ups) | Medium | Builds reputation; few readers hold gold |
| Product Hunt | Medium to low | One day of global reach; upvote culture fits a calm brand badly. Only after Android is confirmed live |
| Show HN | Low to medium | The privacy angle fits; the gold angle doesn't; results vary |
| Apple Search Ads | Optional, only with a budget | Apple's own reporting, nothing added to the app. Only after a baseline exists |
| Influencers, giveaways, paid promotions | Doesn't fit | Claims risk, cost, and it reads as hype |
